Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

KuralIsai plays music from your own Plex Media Server. We collect nothing. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and we run no server that receives your data. This page explains what stays on your device and what the app sends, and to whom.

What stays on your device

  • Your Plex sign-in token, and any AI key you add, in the iPhone/iPad Keychain, on this device only.
  • Your settings, playlist drafts, recent plays and downloaded music.
  • Sign out removes the Plex token. Deleting the app removes everything else.

What the app sends, and to whom

WhenWhatGoes to
You sign inYour approval of KuralIsai on plex.tvPlex
You browse and playRequests for your library and audio, ratings and playlist changes you makeYour own Plex server, and plex.tv to find it
You play on a Sonos or Cast speakerThe address of the audio on your server, with a temporary access code for the server (never your account sign-in), and the track's title, artist and coverThe speaker, on your home network. Cast devices are made by Google and handle what they receive under Google's policies.

You use Sonic Travels, after tapping AllowWhat you type, and the names of your library's genres, styles, moods and decades. No track lists, listening history, Plex sign-in or server details.The AI provider you chose (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google or another service), using your own account and key, under that provider's terms

You can stop sharing with an AI provider at any time in Settings → Sonic Travels, and remove your key there.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or receive your information, and we do not build profiles.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code.

Children

KuralIsai is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.
